## Platform

web

## Users

- **Primary Users:** Bangladeshi and South Asian prospective students planning undergraduate or postgraduate studies abroad in 42+ destinations. They need reliable admission criteria, realistic living costs converted to Bangladeshi Taka (BDT), verified English exam guidelines, and clear visa intelligence.
- **Secondary Users:** Student contributors participating in community corrections and financial document reviews.
- **Administrative Users:** Super Admins, Admins, and Data Editors who govern institutional ingestion, manage candidate queues, verify English test policies, and audit community-submitted corrections.

## Product Purpose

Borno Higher Study provides an authoritative, uncompromised global post-secondary education discovery and governance platform. It eliminates predatory misinformation, outdated requirements, and hidden costs by connecting students to verified university data, deadlines, genuine living expense estimates, and official immigration guidelines.

## Positioning

Uncompromising data integrity and localization: unlike generic lead-generation or affiliate study-abroad aggregators, Borno Higher Study guarantees strictly verified institutional data (zero fabricated universities), non-inferred test acceptance rules (e.g., differentiating in-person test-centre IELTS/TOEFL from at-home editions for visa validity), transparent exhaustiveness indicators for smaller nations, and localized BDT financial intelligence.

## Operating Context

- Evaluated across desktop and mobile browsers by students researching university programs, comparing test formats, evaluating MOI certificates, and building application roadmaps.
- High-trust research environment where inaccurate dates or misinterpreted language waiver policies could cost applicants thousands of dollars or cause visa rejections.
- Super Admin operations running administrative review queues, coverage verification dashboards, and immutable audit logs.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- **Multi-Tier Global Directory:** Universities, colleges, business schools, institutes of technology, and pathway providers indexed across 42+ destination countries with qualification tracking (Bachelor's, Master's, Doctoral, Foundation/Pathway).
- **Institution Ingestion & Deduplication:** 100+ institution milestone for major nations and transparent disclaimers for exhaustive national directories under 100 higher education institutions; 3-tier duplicate detection (Domain, Name, WHED IAU).
- **English Test Intelligence:** Side-by-side comparison of 7 test formats (IELTS Academic, IELTS for UKVI, PTE Academic, PTE Online, PTE UKVI, TOEFL iBT, TOEFL Home Edition) with verified Bangladesh pricing, delivery details, and university-level acceptance rules with non-inference enforcement.
- **Medium of Instruction (MOI) Directory & Checker:** Database of university MOI policies and exemptions (degree duration, graduation validity recency, certificate wording requirements), interactive 4-outcome eligibility checker, and explicit disclaimer that academic MOI does not guarantee visa clearance.
- **Deadlines & Application Intakes:** Intake countdowns, timezone-aware deadline tracking, ICS calendar exports.
- **Community Governance & Audit:** Public correction proposal flows, student financial document verification, and administrative audit trails.
- **Technical Architecture:** React 18, Vite, Tailwind CSS, TypeScript, Framer Motion/GSAP frontend; Node.js/Express, Prisma ORM, and SQLite backend.

## Product Principles

1. **Truth Before Volume:** Never invent or extrapolate institutions, fees, or deadlines to satisfy an arbitrary quota. Transparently disclose verified limits.
2. **Strict Non-Inference:** Acceptance of one exam format (e.g., in-person IELTS/TOEFL) or academic MOI never implies acceptance of online formats or immigration visa compliance.
3. **Student-First Local Utility:** Provide actionable, localized clarity (BDT currency conversions, Bangladesh test centers, local high-school/undergraduate diploma recognition).
4. **Community Scrutiny with Administrative Rigor:** Enable crowdsourced reporting while requiring immutable audit logs and administrative validation before publishing corrections.
